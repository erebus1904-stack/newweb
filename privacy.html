No Account Registration

LicenseAtlas currently does not require account registration. We do not ask users to create a username, password, profile, or paid account to use the practice features.

Local Study Data

Practice progress, answer counts, accuracy, missed-question markers, and recent drill scores are stored locally in your browser using localStorage. This information is not uploaded to our server by the current static version of the site.

You can clear local study data from the app interface, or by clearing your browser storage.

Email Contact

If you contact us by email, we will use your email address and message only to respond to your request. Contact is voluntary. You can reach us at Erebus11@163.com.

Advertising and Third Parties

LicenseAtlas may include Google advertising scripts. Google advertising scripts may use cookies or similar technologies to provide, measure, and improve ads, subject to Google's own policies and controls.

We do not sell personal account data because the current version does not collect account registration data.
