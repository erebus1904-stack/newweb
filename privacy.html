No Account Registration

LicenseAtlas PMP currently does not require account registration. We do not ask users to create a username, password, profile, or paid account to use the project management exam practice features.

Local Study Data

Practice progress, answer counts, accuracy, missed-question markers, and recent mock exam scores are stored locally in your browser using localStorage. This project management exam practice information is not uploaded to our server by the current static version of the site.

You can clear local study data from the app interface, or by clearing your browser storage.

Email Contact

If you send feedback or contact us by email, we will use your email address and message only to respond to your request. Contact is voluntary. You can reach us at erebus1904@protonmail.com.
