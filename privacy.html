Privacy Policy

Your learning progress stays in your browser.

Last updated: June 3, 2026

No Account Registration

PassGrid currently does not require account registration. We do not ask users to create a username, password, profile, or paid account to use the project management exam practice features.

Local Study Data

Practice progress, answer counts, accuracy, missed-question markers, and recent practice scores are stored locally in your browser using localStorage. This project management practice information is not uploaded to our server by the current static version of the site.

You can clear local study data from the app interface, or by clearing your browser storage.

Because local study data stays on the device and browser you use, it may not appear on another device, another browser, or a private browsing session. Clearing site data, using browser cleanup tools, or resetting browser storage may remove saved practice history.

Email Contact

If you send feedback or contact us by email, we will use your email address and message only to respond to your request. Contact is voluntary. You can reach us at erebus1904@protonmail.com.

Advertising and Third Parties

PassGrid may include Google advertising scripts. Google advertising scripts may use cookies or similar technologies to provide, measure, and improve ads, subject to Google's own policies and controls.

Google and third-party vendors may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or other identifiers to serve ads, limit repeated ads, measure ad performance, and help detect invalid activity.

You can read how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

We do not sell personal account data because the current version does not collect account registration data.

Analytics

PassGrid uses Google Analytics to understand broad site usage, such as which study pages are visited, whether practice pages load correctly, and which devices or browsers may need layout improvements. Analytics data is used to improve the learning experience and maintain the site.

Analytics tools may collect technical information such as approximate location, browser, device type, page URL, referral source, and interaction events. PassGrid does not use this information to create user accounts or personally grade individual learners.

Your Choices

You can control cookies and local storage through your browser settings. You can also use browser privacy controls, advertising settings, or extensions to limit tracking where supported. Some site features, such as saved missed questions and local progress review, may stop working if local storage is blocked.
