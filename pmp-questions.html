PMP practice map

Move from PMBOK chapter repair to mixed PMP scenario judgment.

The PMP bank shares a project management knowledge foundation with CAPM, but PMP practice should emphasize situational judgment. Read the full scenario, identify your role and the delivery approach, separate the immediate issue from its symptoms, and choose the next disciplined action. After answering, explain why the other options are weaker in that context. Use the domain counts as navigation, not as a study-time formula: your own error patterns should decide where the next practice block goes.

Chapter practice first

Use PMP chapter practice when several misses point to one knowledge area, such as integration, scope, schedule, cost, quality, resources, communications, risk, procurement, or stakeholders. Work in a short chapter block, write down the decision rule behind each answer, and then use the PMP study prompts to turn weak concepts into a focused review plan. Return to mixed domains only after you can apply the repaired concept in a new scenario.

Domain practice next

Use the domain cards above when you need mixed questions across People, Process, and Business Environment. A mixed set tests whether you can recognize the governing issue without a chapter label giving away the topic. Review role boundaries, tailoring, value delivery, team leadership, compliance, and stakeholder decisions. Track the reason for each miss—knowledge gap, rushed reading, role confusion, or poor sequencing—so the next session addresses a pattern instead of merely adding question volume.

2026 PMP emphasis

Read the PMP 2026 exam-version guide before choosing a target date or interpreting domain labels. It explains how to distinguish the exam outline from this site's practice inventory. Review value delivery, benefits, compliance, organizational change, sustainability, and AI-related project judgment when those misses repeat, but do not infer official exam probabilities from PassGrid's category totals. Recheck the source guide when your preparation crosses an exam-version transition.

Recommended next step

After domain practice, open the 180-question PMP mock exam when you can protect a full timed attempt and a separate review block. Do not stop at the score. Group misses by domain, decision pattern, and time pressure, then use the PMP mistake-review prompts to challenge your reasoning and create corrective drills. A mock is most useful when its evidence changes the next study session; repeated attempts without analysis can hide the same weakness behind a new percentage.
