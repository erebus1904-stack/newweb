PMP practice map

Move from PMBOK chapter repair to mixed PMP scenario judgment.

The PMP bank shares the same project management knowledge foundation as CAPM, but the PMP path should be used differently: read the scenario, identify the role and delivery approach, choose the next disciplined action, and review the decision rule behind every miss.

Chapter practice first

Use PMP chapter practice when misses point to one PMBOK area such as integration, scope, schedule, cost, quality, resources, communications, risk, procurement, or stakeholders.

Domain practice next

Use the domain cards above when you need mixed questions across People, Process, and Business Environment. This is where role judgment, tailoring, value delivery, and stakeholder decisions become more important than term recognition.

2026 PMP emphasis

The 2026 outline increases Business Environment weight to 26%. Review value delivery, benefits, compliance, organizational change, sustainability, and AI-related project judgment when those misses repeat.

Recommended next step

After domain practice, open the 180-question PMP mock exam only when you can protect four hours for the attempt and at least one review block for explanations and mistake patterns.
