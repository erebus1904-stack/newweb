Mock exam method

Use the mock exam after chapter practice, not before it.

This timed PMP mock exam is meant to test exam rhythm after you have already reviewed the major PMP reference topics, practiced chapter-level questions, and corrected repeated mistakes.

Work under the 230-minute limit

The timer continues from the moment the exam session starts. If you leave the page and return later in the same browser, the saved start time is used, so the remaining time reflects the real elapsed time.

Pacing checkpoints

Use rough checkpoints instead of staring at the clock after every question. For a 180-question set under 230 minutes, check whether you are near question 60 around the first 75 minutes and near question 120 around the first 150 minutes, then reserve the final block for unanswered and marked questions.

Change answers before submission

Use the question list to return to marked or uncertain questions. The exam does not reveal correctness while you are answering, because the point is to practice pacing, review discipline, and final decision confidence.

Return to domain practice if pacing collapses

If the mock result shows slow reading or repeated uncertainty in one domain, return to smaller PMP domain practice before starting another full simulation.

Use chapter practice for repeated gaps

When misses cluster around a PMBOK topic, repair that chapter first. Chapter practice is faster than repeating a full mock when the problem is one weak concept area.

Review results by pattern

After submission, compare misses by topic and decision type: stakeholder communication, change control, risk response, agile roles, business environment, procurement, and next-best-action judgment.

Use a prompt after submission

After a mock exam, open the PMP mistake review prompts and summarize misses by decision pattern before starting another full mock.

Use a prompt before final booking

Before turning mock readiness into an appointment decision, use the PMP exam operations prompts to organize scheduling, delivery format, ID, room, travel, and official PMI or Pearson VUE checks.

Connect misses to guides

When a missed question points to a repeated gap, use the PMP guides for next-best-action questions, change control, or agile, hybrid, and predictive signals before repeating another mock.

Check the exam version you are preparing for

If your exam date is near the July 2026 update, compare your mock review against the current exam-version guide before deciding whether a missed area is a local weakness or a version-weighting issue.

Do not treat one score as the whole story

A single mock result is useful only when it tells you what to review next. If the score is weak, return to the PMP Study Hub, rebuild the matching reference topic, and repeat smaller question sets before starting another full simulation. Save the full mock for moments when you can protect uninterrupted review time and focused scoring review.
