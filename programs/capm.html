Practice bank structure

The CAPM workspace opens all 1000 questions so beginners can read explanations immediately and connect terms to realistic project situations.

The practice bank follows the CAPM domain mix across Project Management Fundamentals and Core Concepts, Predictive and Plan-Based Methodologies, Agile Frameworks and Methodologies, and Business Analysis Frameworks. The intended loop is short: answer a question, read the reason, and save missed concepts for later review.

CAPM domain weights

  • Project Management Fundamentals and Core Concepts - 36%: Learn project, program, portfolio, stakeholder, deliverable, risk, issue, assumption, constraint, lifecycle, and role language first.
  • Predictive, Plan-Based Methodologies - 17%: Study scope, WBS, schedule logic, cost baseline, quality, formal acceptance, and change control as one managed delivery system.
  • Agile Frameworks and Methodologies - 20%: Know Scrum roles, backlog priority, Daily Scrum, Sprint Review, Retrospective, Kanban flow, WIP limits, and iterative feedback.
  • Business Analysis Frameworks - 27%: Connect stakeholder needs to requirements, traceability, acceptance criteria, product scope, and project scope.

30-day and 45-day study paths

  • 30-day path: Spend week one on fundamentals, week two on predictive and agile, week three on business analysis and mixed practice, and week four on timed sets, missed questions, and exam-day checks.
  • 45-day path: Use the first 15 days for concepts and required education hours, the next 15 days for domain-based practice, and the final 15 days for mixed sets, timing, weak-area repair, and final review sheets.
  • Daily loop: Read or watch one focused topic, answer a small practice set, tag every miss by cause, and write one rule you can reuse on the next set.

How to use Practice and Mock Exam

  • Practice mode: Use it while learning. Read the explanation immediately, then decide whether the miss came from a concept gap, missed keyword, delivery approach confusion, business analysis scenario, or English terminology.
  • Mock Exam mode: Use it after you can explain the basics. Treat timing as part of the skill because CAPM has 150 questions in 180 minutes.
  • Mistake review: Do not memorize answers. Retest weak categories until the reason for the answer is clear without rereading the full explanation.

CAPM vs PMP

  • CAPM: Best for beginners who need project management language, process awareness, role clarity, and confidence reading exam questions.
  • PMP: Best for experienced professionals who lead or manage projects and must choose the next best action in realistic scenarios.
  • Study order: If you have limited project experience, start with CAPM fundamentals. If you already manage project constraints and stakeholders, move into PMP scenario practice.

When to move toward PMP

Move from CAPM fundamentals toward PMP practice when the terms feel familiar and you can explain why a project manager should analyze impact, communicate with the right stakeholder, use change control, remove impediments, or escalate only when authority is exceeded.
