PMP exam domains

The practice bank is organized around the PMI PMP Examination Content Outline and its practical decision areas.

  • People: conflict, team development, stakeholder engagement, servant leadership, negotiation, and facilitation.
  • Process: change control, risk response, schedule, scope, quality, procurement, communications, and delivery governance.
  • Business Environment: benefits, compliance, organizational change, strategic alignment, and value delivery.
  • Agile, hybrid, and predictive delivery: choosing responses that fit the project life cycle instead of applying one method everywhere.

How to answer PMP situational questions

Most PMP scenarios reward disciplined project management behavior. Before choosing an answer, identify the delivery approach, the stakeholder involved, the constraint, and whether the situation is a risk, issue, change, conflict, or communication gap.

  • Prefer collaboration before escalation when the project manager can resolve the issue with the team or stakeholder.
  • Use formal change control when approved scope, schedule, cost, or baselines may change.
  • Do not ignore constraints stated in the question. The best answer usually responds to the actual condition, not a generic best practice.
  • Match the life cycle before selecting the action. Agile, hybrid, and predictive projects use different control points.

Sample explanation style

Scenario: A key stakeholder asks the team to add work during the sprint because the request is urgent.

Best response: Clarify the request, protect the current sprint goal, and route the item through the product owner or agreed change path depending on the delivery approach.

Why: PMP questions often test whether the project manager respects the governance model. Acting immediately may create uncontrolled scope change, while refusing without discussion may damage stakeholder engagement.
