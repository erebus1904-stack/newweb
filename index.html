Common questions

What to know before using PassGrid.

Do I need to sign up to try the questions?

No. Practice questions do not require a login. Progress is stored in your browser, so it may not transfer to another device. The free study-material request only asks for email because the materials need to be sent to you.

What does PassGrid provide for PMP and CAPM study?

PassGrid supports more than question practice. It includes PMP and CAPM learning paths, knowledge-point explanations, focused practice under each knowledge area, answer explanations, mistake review, and study-guide articles. It is built for exam review and self-study, not for issuing official PMI education credits.

Why is PassGrid free?

PassGrid is free because we want more PMP and CAPM candidates to have access to useful practice, study guidance, and review materials without paying before they know what they need. To keep the site available and maintained, we may use advertising to help cover operating costs. Thank you for using the site and supporting that model.

How should I use PMP and CAPM together?

Start with CAPM if you need definitions and foundations. Use PMP practice when you need judgment-heavy scenario decisions.

Is PassGrid affiliated with PMI?

No. PassGrid is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by PMI.
