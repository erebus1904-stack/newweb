Editorial Policy

How PassGrid creates and reviews CAPM and PMP study content.

Last updated: June 26, 2026

Scope

PassGrid publishes independent study guides, practice questions, explanations, and review workflows for CAPM and PMP learners. The site does not publish official PMI exam questions and does not claim affiliation with, endorsement by, or operation by Project Management Institute.

Content is limited to project management exam preparation so the site stays focused on one learning category instead of acting as a broad credential directory.

Source Basis

When a page discusses exam structure, eligibility, domains, online testing, or candidate rules, the article is written against current public PMI or Pearson VUE information where available. Because official requirements can change, readers should verify final exam policies with the official provider before paying for an exam, scheduling a test, or choosing a course.

Study advice pages convert official topic areas into practical learning workflows, such as mistake review, time allocation, scenario-question reasoning, and practice pacing.

Practice Question Standards

Practice questions are written as original learning prompts. They are designed to test project management concepts, role boundaries, delivery approaches, business analysis logic, risk, change, stakeholder communication, and next-step reasoning. They are not copied from PMI exams, prep books, or paid courses.

Explanations should help learners understand the rule being tested, not only memorize a letter choice. When questions are reviewed, priority is given to reducing repeated wording, clarifying why a response is correct, and removing prompts that feel mechanical or too similar to nearby items.

Updates and Corrections

Pages are updated when exam information changes, when content becomes unclear, or when user feedback identifies a weak explanation. Corrections can be sent to erebus1904@protonmail.com.

Feedback is reviewed for factual issues, unclear wording, broken links, outdated official references, and practice questions that do not match the intended domain or delivery approach.

Independence

PassGrid is an independent learning aid. CAPM, PMP, PMI, PMBOK, Pearson VUE, and related names are used only to identify exam categories, study topics, and official sources that candidates should check directly.
