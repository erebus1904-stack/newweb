PMI APL vs PMP: Eligibility, Exam and Current Limits

APL is now an official PMI credential, but its route is not simply "PMP plus another exam." Compare what is required before investing in preparation.

Last reviewed: October 26, 2026Published by PassGrid Editorial Team.Editorial Policy

Short answer

Advanced Project Leader (APL) is PMI's certification for experienced professionals leading complex, high-stakes work. Unlike PMP, its current route requires an active PMP, PgMP or PfMP in good standing and successful completion of an APL evaluation program at a PMI-accredited organization. Holding PMP alone does not establish APL eligibility.

What is PMI APL?

PMI announced Advanced Project Leader on October 22, 2026, at PMI Global Summit in Detroit. PMI describes it as recognition of demonstrated leadership capability in complex, consequential project settings. "Advanced PMP" was an informal label used in earlier discussion; use Advanced Project Leader (APL) when checking present requirements.

This launch changes the status of older pilot coverage. APL is no longer merely an unnamed proposal, although PMI's current candidate route is still organization-dependent.

Who is eligible for APL?

Confirmed by PMI: candidates need an active PMP, PgMP or PfMP certification in good standing and must successfully complete an APL evaluation program at a PMI-accredited organization. CAPM is not on PMI's current list of qualifying credentials. PMP is therefore one route into APL, not the only qualifying credential.

PMI accredits organizations' evaluation programs to assess advanced practice with fairness, rigor and consistency. An ordinary preparation course is not the same as completing that accredited evaluation. PMI's candidate page says no separate experience audit or additional panel review is required beyond its published route; do not confuse those with the organization's evaluation.

How does the current application pathway work?

  1. Check that your PMP, PgMP or PfMP credential is active and in good standing.
  2. Ask your employer's PMO, learning team or professional development lead whether it has a PMI-accredited APL evaluation program and whether you can participate.
  3. Request the program's assessment criteria, evidence requirements, schedule and costs. Complete its evaluation before treating yourself as exam-eligible.
  4. After eligibility is confirmed, use PMI's current instructions for exam registration, payment and scheduling.

If your organization has no accredited program, PMI offers organization accreditation information. Joining an interest list or buying a course is not the same as eligibility approval. Confirm your personal circumstances directly with PMI and the participating organization.

What does the APL exam assess?

PMI describes technical-knowledge questions, situational-judgment questions and case studies. Its published competency areas span leadership and influence; communication and collaboration; engagement; strategic and systems thinking; problem-solving; risk and uncertainty management; agility and responsiveness; and cultural and ecosystem intelligence. Use PMI's current exam information and Exam Content Outline to select study materials rather than assuming a PMP question bank fully covers APL.

Current limits to understand

  • Access: a qualifying credential does not provide a direct individual route without the accredited organizational evaluation.
  • Costs: ask separately about organizational assessment, preparation, exam registration and renewal. Do not assume a course advertisement includes every charge.
  • Career outcomes: a new credential's launch does not establish a salary premium, promotion probability or employer demand. Compare it with the work and roles you actually pursue.
  • Older guidance: articles calling APL only an unnamed pilot predate PMI's October 22 announcement and need rechecking.

Frequently asked questions

Is APL replacing PMP?

No replacement announcement appears in the PMI sources reviewed here. PMI presents them as separate credentials with different routes.

Can I apply independently?

PMI's currently published eligibility route requires completion of a program at a PMI-accredited organization. Check with PMI before assuming an independent route exists.

Do I have to take PMP first?

No. Active PgMP or PfMP also qualifies as the prior credential; you still need the accredited evaluation. CAPM alone is not listed.

Does passing a preparation course make me eligible?

No. A course is not a substitute for the required accredited organizational evaluation.

Is APL worth pursuing?

That depends on whether you lead work matching APL's scope, can access an accredited program, and see relevant employer recognition. Verify total cost and requirements before committing.
