PMP Exam Accommodations: Request Steps and Supporting Documents

Plan the request around PMI's application and payment sequence, then confirm your individual testing arrangements before scheduling.

Published: September 23, 2026Last reviewed: September 23, 2026By PassGrid Editorial Team.Editorial Policy

How do I request PMP exam accommodations?

PMI's exam accommodations process starts with an approved certification application. During payment, indicate that you need accommodations. PMI then emails instructions for submitting its request form and supporting documentation. Apply early and wait for PMI's decision before making an exam booking that depends on an adjustment; a diagnosis alone does not guarantee a specific arrangement.

Follow the official request sequence

PMI's current public process page sets the order. A candidate first meets the exam's eligibility criteria and submits a certification application. PMI reviews that application and makes an approval decision. That approval lets you proceed with the accommodation request; it is not itself an accommodation approval.

  1. Read the guidelines: Open PMI's Guidelines for Requesting Exam Accommodations before choosing what to request or arranging an evaluation.
  2. Pay through the PMI process: During online certification payment, indicate that you require exam accommodations. Do not assume a generic appointment note replaces this step.
  3. Watch for PMI's email: After payment, PMI sends instructions for the request and supporting documentation. Follow those instructions and the linked form rather than a forum template.
  4. Submit privately: Complete and sign the Accommodation Request Form and send it with your supporting documents through PMI's Contact Us route.
  5. Confirm the outcome: Ask PMI which arrangement was approved and which delivery options and appointment steps apply to your specific case.

PassGrid planning advice: keep the PMI email, a copy of the form, submission confirmation, and the written decision together. Submit as early as you reasonably can if your preferred exam date matters. These personal records help you track a request; PMI's instructions control the actual submission.

What supporting documentation does PMI request?

PMI's guidelines, especially pages 4 and 7, connect the requested arrangement to a documented functional limitation. The evaluation should explain how the condition affects access to testing under standard conditions and why the requested adjustment addresses that limitation. A diagnostic label on its own is insufficient. PMI decides each request individually.

Allow time for review before choosing an exam date

The PMI guidelines, page 5, say PMI will make every attempt to process a request within 30 days after all supporting documents are received. Requests needing more information may take longer. The 30-day statement is a processing aim, not a guaranteed approval date or a promise that every request will be granted.

The same guidelines tell candidates to notify PMI before registering to take an exam. Build your calendar around the approved arrangement and appointment instructions, especially if travel, a test center, or a particular time slot is involved. Do not assume the standard 240-minute PMP exam duration applies unchanged to your approved arrangement.

If an exam is already scheduled, contact PMI before changing the booking. Its public page lists this as a question, but the visible page does not provide an answer that covers every case. Ask what to do with your existing appointment and whether any scheduling or rescheduling conditions apply. Keep the answer in writing.

Check online and test-center availability for your arrangement

PMI's guidelines, page 6, say that candidates who require certain accommodations are not eligible for online proctored testing and must use a test center. The public text does not provide a universal list that lets PassGrid determine which delivery method you can use. Confirm your approved arrangement and delivery route with PMI before booking an online appointment.

This matters if you have already arranged a quiet home workspace. A passed OnVUE system check tests equipment, not accommodation approval. Use our online versus test-center guide to compare ordinary delivery requirements after PMI clarifies your options. Regular exam breaks and any individually approved extra breaks are distinct; follow the terms PMI communicates for your appointment.

Common questions

When should I request accommodations?

Start the certification application first. After PMI approves it, read the guidelines, flag the need during payment, and respond to PMI's document request. Submit early enough for review before the exam date you hope to book. The official guidance calls for notifying PMI before registering for an exam appointment.

Does an ADHD diagnosis guarantee extra time?

No. PMI's guidelines say diagnosis alone does not establish entitlement to an accommodation. The documentation must explain current functional limitations and why a particular adjustment is needed to access the test. PMI considers the request individually. Extra time is one possible arrangement, not an automatic benefit for any diagnosis.

What if I had accommodations on another PMI exam?

The guidelines, page 5, say to request the arrangement again and provide the earlier PMI approval notice. If the supporting documents are no longer current, PMI may require an updated request. Do not assume a prior approval transfers automatically.

Can I take an unscheduled break?

Do not infer permission from another candidate's experience. Ask PMI whether your approved arrangement includes additional breaks and follow your appointment's proctor instructions. The standard PMP break schedule does not determine an individual's approved accommodation.
