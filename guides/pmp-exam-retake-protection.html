PMP Exam Retake Protection Is Official: What PMI Confirms and What Remains Unclear

PMI now has a first-party product page, but candidates still need to verify the live checkout terms before treating reported prices or purchase restrictions as policy.

Published and reviewed: August 28, 2026By PassGrid Editorial Team.Editorial Policy

Short answer

PMI now has an official PMP Exam Retake Protection product page, confirming that the product exists. The public PMI page does not reliably show its price, eligibility rules, purchase window, or coverage terms. Verify those details in your PMI account or checkout before paying, and treat support reports as case-specific evidence rather than official policy.

What PMI officially confirms

PMI's shop lists a product called Project Management Professional (PMP) Exam Retake Protection, offered by PMI under product code CE045. This is enough to confirm that Retake Protection is a real PMI product rather than a rumor or an unaffiliated insurance offer.

The public page is not yet a reliable source for the complete commercial terms. During our August 28 review, key fields displayed as "Loading," while unrelated course-module content also appeared on the page. A search result or page title should therefore not be stretched into claims about exactly who qualifies, how long protection lasts, or what happens in every cancellation scenario.

Can you add PMP Retake Protection after paying for the exam?

There is not enough public first-party information to give a universal yes or no. One candidate report says PMI support would not simply add the product after exam payment and instead described cancellation and rebooking. That report is useful as a warning about timing, but it is a single support interaction and not an official published rule.

The practical response is to look for Retake Protection during the original payment flow. If it is missing or the terms are unclear, contact PMI before completing payment. Ask for the answer in writing and compare any cancellation fee, lost appointment, eligibility window, or price change before altering an existing booking. Do not cancel a paid exam solely because a forum post says rebooking is required.

What the product should not be confused with

Retake Protection should not be described as a guaranteed pass, a second PMP credential, or permission to ignore PMI's normal exam rules. It also should not be assumed to return the original exam fee. The product name suggests protection related to a retake, but only the terms presented by PMI at purchase can define the covered event and remedy.

PMI already has general eligibility, scheduling, cancellation, and retake policies. A protection product may interact with those policies without replacing all of them. Save the version of the terms you accept so you can identify which rules applied to your transaction.

Checklist before you buy

  1. Sign in first. Regional and membership pricing may depend on the account completing the purchase.
  2. Confirm the covered event. Check whether coverage applies only after a failed first attempt and exactly what benefit follows.
  3. Check the purchase deadline. Determine whether it must be added with the original exam payment.
  4. Read the exclusions. Look for absence, cancellation, rescheduling, exam-security action, eligibility expiration, and other events that may not be covered.
  5. Check validity and location. Confirm the period, region, delivery mode, and exam version to which the product applies.
  6. Save evidence. Keep the receipt, terms, confirmation email, and screenshots of the checkout description.
  7. Compare the downside. Evaluate the product price against the cost and likelihood of a retake, without weakening your preparation plan.

Is PMP Retake Protection worth it?

The answer depends on verified price, coverage, and your tolerance for the financial risk of a second attempt. It may be attractive when the stated benefit is clear, the product is available at the initial checkout, and the protection price is materially lower than the covered retake cost. It may offer little value when an exclusion applies, an existing booking would have to be cancelled at a cost, or the terms are not clear enough to show what you receive.

Protection should change financial risk, not study standards. Use the current PMP Examination Content Outline, timed practice, and a realistic readiness review. A product that reduces one possible retake expense does not reduce the time, stress, or eligibility constraints attached to another attempt.

Common questions

Is the reported member or nonmember price official?

Not for every candidate. The reported $45 and $61 figures come from a secondary article, while PMI's public page did not expose a stable price during our review. The amount in your signed-in PMI checkout is the relevant amount.

Does Retake Protection guarantee a free second exam?

Do not assume that wording. Confirm the covered benefit, conditions, exclusions, and redemption process in the terms presented with your purchase.

Should an already-booked candidate cancel and rebook?

Not based on a community report alone. Ask PMI how the product applies to your transaction, calculate cancellation and scheduling effects, and retain the response before changing the booking.
