Can a $20 Udemy Course Really Count for 60 PMP PDUs?

A low price can be legitimate, but the listing headline is not the rule. Audit the learning hours, Talent Triangle allocation, reporting path, and evidence before you buy or claim PDUs.

Published and reviewed: August 10, 2026By PassGrid Editorial Team.Editorial Policy

Short answer

A low-cost Udemy course can support a 60-PDU PMP renewal claim, but price and platform do not make the PDUs valid. The completed learning must be relevant, match PMI's Talent Triangle categories, reflect actual learning time, be reported correctly in the Certification Requirements System (CCRS), and be supported by records if PMI audits the claim.

The official 60-PDU PMP renewal rules

A PMP holder needs 60 professional development units during each three-year Continuing Certification Requirements (CCR) cycle. At least 35 must be Education PDUs. The three Talent Triangle areas each have an eight-PDU minimum, leaving the remaining 11 Education PDUs flexible across any of the three areas. Giving Back is optional, not required.

PMI permits claims in increments as small as 0.25 PDU. The general calculation is one PDU for one hour spent in a qualifying professional-development activity. This makes course duration important, but duration alone is not enough: the content must also be relevant and allocated correctly.

Do not confuse renewal PDUs with the 35 training hours for a PMP application

The similar numbers create a common mistake. The 35 training hours used for PMP application eligibility are completed before certification and support an exam application. The minimum 35 Education PDUs are earned after certification during a CCR renewal cycle. Training completed before earning the credential cannot simply be reused as renewal PDUs.

A course marketed for PMP renewal should therefore be evaluated as continuing education. It should not be assumed to satisfy the separate education requirement for a first-time PMP application unless PMI's current eligibility rules and the provider's documentation also support that use.

Can a Udemy or other third-party course count?

Yes, potentially. PMI's CCR guidance says third-party provider courses can count when the activity is relevant and meets the CCR criteria. Online and digital learning are also recognized Education paths. The provider does not have to be the PMI marketplace for every qualifying activity, and a low promotional price does not make the learning invalid.

But a third-party course is not automatically approved merely because its title says "60 PDUs," displays the PMI or PMP terms, includes a completion certificate, or provides claim instructions. The credential holder is responsible for reporting the activity accurately. If a provider reports PDUs on the holder's behalf, the holder should still verify the CCRS record and avoid submitting the same activity again.

Why a $20 price tells you almost nothing

Marketplace prices change by promotion, account, and region. Price is neither a PMI acceptance rule nor a reliable proxy for learning hours. Instead, compare the advertised total with the lectures, bundled courses, external learning, or professional-work claims the learner must complete.

If visible lectures total fewer than 60 hours, do not claim 60 Education PDUs from those lectures alone. Identify every additional qualifying activity and its category. Professional work may support Work as a Practitioner PDUs, but it is not Education or purchased course time.

Course audit checklist before you buy

  1. Confirm the audience. Check whether it is for PMP renewal, first-time application training, or both.
  2. Reconcile the hours. Compare the PDU total with lectures, required activities, bundled courses, and external learning.
  3. Inspect the category split. Require a written allocation for Ways of Working, Power Skills, and Business Acumen. Check that all three reach at least eight PDUs.
  4. Read the course outline. The syllabus should show how the topics relate to project management and the current PMP Exam Content Outline.
  5. Identify the reporting path. Determine whether the provider reports through CCRS or supplies self-reporting details.
  6. Check the evidence. Require a completion certificate, dates, learning time, provider, title, and category breakdown.
  7. Reject double counting. The same course or activity cannot be claimed more than once.

How to report the activity in CCRS

Use PMI's Certification Requirements System (CCRS) to record and monitor PDUs. The exact interface can change, but the reporting logic remains the same:

  1. Sign in with the PMI account connected to the active PMP credential.
  2. Choose the Education activity type that accurately describes the course or online learning.
  3. Enter the provider, course title, description, activity dates, and qualifying hours.
  4. Allocate the hours across Ways of Working, Power Skills, and Business Acumen according to the course content.
  5. Review the claim before submitting it, then retain the confirmation and supporting records.

If the provider says it will report the course automatically, wait for the activity to appear and verify the title, dates, total, and category allocation. Do not create a second self-reported claim for the same learning.

What to keep in case PMI audits the claim

PMI may select PDU claims for audit. For course or training activity, the handbook identifies a registration form, completion certificate, or attendance letter as supporting documentation. For online and digital learning, keep evidence that supports the learning activity, including notes and activity dates.

Keep the receipt, completion certificate, course outline or syllabus, category breakdown, learning notes, dates, and CCRS confirmation for at least 18 months after the CCR cycle ends. A saved listing can document the offer, but it does not replace completion evidence.

Frequently asked questions

Does Udemy price affect whether PMI accepts a PDU claim?

No. PMI's CCR rules focus on the learning activity, relevance, hours, category allocation, reporting, and evidence. A $20 promotion and a higher list price are treated the same under those criteria.

Does a completion certificate guarantee 60 PDUs?

No. A completion certificate is useful audit evidence, but it does not prove that every advertised PDU was earned or allocated correctly. Reconcile the certificate with the actual activities and course outline.

Can all 60 PMP renewal PDUs be Education?

Yes. PMI sets a minimum of 35 Education PDUs, not a maximum. All 60 may come from qualifying Education activities as long as the Talent Triangle minimums are met.
