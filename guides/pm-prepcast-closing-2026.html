PM PrepCast Is Closing December 4, 2026: What PMP and CAPM Students Should Save

Current customers have a limited window to complete courses, download certificates, preserve simulator records, and verify which unresolved closure arrangements apply to them.

Published and reviewed: September 1, 2026By PassGrid Editorial Team.Editorial Policy

Short answer

PM PrepCast and OSP International say their products, systems, and account access will close on December 4, 2026. October 31 is the last day to purchase, and current course users are advised to finish by November 30 so they can complete the assessment and download a certificate. Save certificates, receipts, simulator summaries, progress records, and downloadable files before access ends. Previously earned training records do not disappear merely because the provider closes, but PMI remains the authority for accepting contact hours or PDUs.

What the official closure FAQ confirms

PM PrepCast's official FAQ says the business is closing after changes in the exam-preparation market made the independent training operation financially unsustainable. The announcement applies to its PMP, CAPM, PMI-ACP, course, and simulator customers. The separate Project Management Podcast is not ending.

Existing users retain access until December 4 or until their current access period ends, whichever comes first. On December 4, customer accounts become inaccessible and course progress, simulator exam history, and personal account data are scheduled for deletion, apart from records the company must retain for legal reasons. Support remains available through the closure date.

What PMP and CAPM course users should save

  1. Completion certificate. Download the final certificate after completing the required lessons and assessment.
  2. Purchase receipt. Keep the provider, product, payment date, and transaction details with the certificate.
  3. Course outline. Save a syllabus or product description showing the covered topics and stated training hours.
  4. Simulator summaries. Print or save full-exam scores, dates, domain results, and any review pages that remain available.
  5. Progress records. Preserve evidence of completed lessons and assessments rather than relying on the account dashboard.
  6. Downloadable files. Save formula guides, PDFs, and other files that the license currently allows users to download.
  7. Support answers. Retain written responses about refunds, extensions, remaining access, or an unusual account case.

A screenshot of a score is useful for personal study history, but it is not a PMP or CAPM application document. Keep certification-related evidence separate from practice analytics so the records have a clear purpose.

Will an earned certificate remain valid?

The official FAQ says a certificate already earned stays valid because it records training completed before the closure. It also says contact hours and PDUs are earned when the training is completed, not according to whether the provider remains in business. Keep both the certificate and receipt for a possible PMI audit.

That statement does not guarantee that every incomplete course, unsupported hour claim, or incorrectly reported PDU will be accepted. PMI controls certification eligibility, CCR reporting, and audits. The practical safeguard is to finish the eligible activity accurately and preserve the evidence while the provider can still answer questions.

What happens to the simulator and exam history?

PM PrepCast says the simulator will not remain accessible after December 4. Candidates whose exams occur later should therefore prioritize full-length simulations and save or print the available score reports and review records before shutdown. Do not plan a December or later study schedule around continued simulator access.

The FAQ says a failed candidate may still request the usual simulator extension, but no extension can run beyond December 4. That makes the benefit limited for a later retake. Verify the actual dates before allowing a nominal extension to replace a study resource that will remain available.

Refund and remaining-access limits

Purchases made before August 31 remain subject to the terms that applied at purchase, including a 30-day guarantee where that guarantee covered the product. Purchases made on or after September 1 are described as final and non-refundable. The FAQ also says there will be no refund or credit simply for access time lost after December 4.

Customers whose paid access would have extended beyond the closure should follow the FAQ for updates. The company says it is working on a fair arrangement and expects more detail by late September. Do not publish or rely on a specific replacement, credit, or delivery method until that update appears.

Will course lessons remain available after closure?

The FAQ says the company is working on a way for customers to retain course lessons, but the delivery method and final terms are not confirmed. Details are expected before December 4. The simulator is explicitly treated differently and should not be assumed to continue.

Plan from the confirmed shutdown rather than the hoped-for solution. Continue the course now, download what is already permitted, and check the official FAQ instead of relying on reposted summaries. A possible future third-party version is also described as exploratory, not promised access for existing customers.

Should a new student still buy PrepCast?

The provider says discounted products remain available until October 31 and advises against buying when the exam is after December 4. A purchase can make sense only when the buyer understands the final-sale term, can use the product before access ends, and does not depend on unresolved post-closure arrangements.

Compare the remaining calendar time with the course length, assessment, planned simulations, exam date, and time needed for review. A discount does not compensate for an unusable access window. Do not describe an alternative simulator as equivalent without checking its current exam alignment, question authorship, access period, explanations, and refund terms.

Common questions

Does PM PrepCast closing cancel a PMP or CAPM application?

No automatic cancellation follows from the provider closure. The relevant issue is whether the candidate completed eligible training and can document it under PMI's current requirements.

Will the Project Management Podcast also close?

No. The closure FAQ says the podcast will continue.

Can account data be retrieved after December 4?

The provider says it will not be able to retrieve deleted customer accounts, course progress, or simulator history after shutdown. Export needed records in advance.
