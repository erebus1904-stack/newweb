Missed-question review

PMP mistake review prompts for missed questions.

Use these prompts after PMP practice questions, chapter practice, or the PMP mock exam. The point is to find why you missed a question, not to memorize the answer. Good review connects each miss back to a decision pattern in the PMP Study Hub.

Copyable prompts

Turn wrong answers into the next review target.

Paste the question summary, your selected answer, the correct answer, the explanation, and why you chose your answer. Do not paste copyrighted full question banks from other providers.

Use case: Classify missed PMP questions by cause.

I am reviewing missed PMP practice questions. Classify each miss by the likely cause: concept gap, delivery approach confusion, stakeholder or communication issue, change control error, risk versus issue confusion, timing error, or careless reading. For each missed question, explain the decision signal I missed, the correct project manager action pattern, and the next practice topic I should review. Do not just restate the correct answer.

What to paste

  • Short question summary and topic.
  • Your answer, correct answer, and explanation notes.
  • Why your selected answer seemed attractive.

Expected output

  • Cause label for each miss.
  • Decision signal you missed.
  • Next practice topic and guide link to review.

Next step

Review next-best-action questions if timing or first-action logic appears often.

Use case: Find repeated weak topics from a practice set.

I completed a PMP practice set and missed several questions. Group the misses by repeated weak topic and decision pattern. Separate isolated mistakes from repeated problems. For each repeated problem, recommend one guide topic, one chapter practice target, and one rule I should write in my review notes.

What to paste

  • Practice set size and score.
  • List of missed topics or short summaries.
  • Any patterns you already suspect.

Expected output

  • Repeated weak topics ranked by importance.
  • One decision rule for each repeated topic.
  • Focused practice recommendation.

Next step

Use PMP chapter practice for the highest-ranked weak topic.

Use case: Turn a wrong answer into a review checklist.

I chose the wrong answer on a PMP situational question. Build a review checklist from this miss. Identify the role, delivery approach, conflict, constraint, first action, and why my selected answer was less appropriate. End with a checklist I can use before choosing an answer on similar questions.

What to paste

  • Scenario summary.
  • Your selected answer and correct answer.
  • Explanation or decision rule from PassGrid.

Expected output

  • Question-reading checklist.
  • Why the wrong answer was tempting.
  • Similar trap to watch for next time.

Next step

Read how to read PMP situational questions, then repeat a small practice set.

Use case: Review a mock exam by decision pattern.

I finished a PMP mock exam. Help me review the result by decision pattern, not just by score. Group my misses into stakeholder communication, change control, risk and issue handling, agile roles, delivery approach selection, business environment, procurement, and careless reading. Recommend what to review before I take another mock exam.

What to paste

  • Mock score and time remaining.
  • Missed-question topics or notes.
  • Questions you marked or changed.

Expected output

  • Miss pattern summary.
  • Retake readiness recommendation.
  • Focused review list before another mock.

Next step

Use the PMP mock exam again only after the highest-risk patterns have a review block.

Use case: Separate concept gaps from keyword traps.

I am not sure whether my PMP misses are concept gaps or keyword traps. Review these missed-question notes and classify each one. For concept gaps, explain the missing idea. For keyword traps, identify the misleading phrase and the better signal. For each miss, give me a one-sentence rule for future questions.

What to paste

  • Missed-question notes with stem keywords.
  • The answer you chose.
  • The explanation summary.

Expected output

  • Concept gap or keyword trap label.
  • Better signal to watch for.
  • One-sentence future rule.

Next step

Review delivery approach signals if agile, hybrid, or predictive clues caused the miss.

Use case: Build a seven-day repair plan after a weak score.

I scored lower than expected on PMP practice. Build a seven-day repair plan using my weak topics, missed-question causes, and available time. Prioritize the smallest set of topics that could improve my next practice result. Include guide review, chapter practice, mixed questions, mistake notes, and a checkpoint for deciding whether to take another mock exam.

What to paste

  • Practice score and question count.
  • Top missed patterns.
  • Available time for the next seven days.

Expected output

  • Seven-day repair schedule.
  • Focused guides and practice targets.
  • Checkpoint before another mock or domain set.

Next step

Use guides for change control or risk and issue questions if those patterns dominate your misses.

Example use

Example input and output summary.

Example input: 20-question Process practice set, 13 correct, missed change control, risk response, and agile role questions. Example output should group misses by cause, identify whether the first action was impact assessment, communication, escalation, or backlog discussion, and recommend a short repair sequence before mixed practice.

Common mistakes

Do not paste only the correct answer and ask why. Do not ask for a new answer key. Do not skip the reason you chose the wrong answer, because that reason often reveals the real pattern.

Best next action

After classification, choose one repeated pattern and practice it. If misses are scattered, return to the PMP study prompts to rebuild a short study plan.
