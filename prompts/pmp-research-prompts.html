Official-source research

PMP research prompts for official sources and exam policy checks.

Use these prompts when you need to check current PMP information instead of relying on memory, old notes, or generic AI answers. The prompt should create a research trail: what to check on PMI, what to check on Pearson VUE, what changed, what remains uncertain, and what you should not act on until the official source is confirmed.

Copyable prompts

Make the model show its source trail.

The safest output is not "the answer is X." The safest output is a table of claims, official source links, last checked dates, conflicts, and unresolved items to verify before you schedule, pay, or change your study plan.

Use case: Check a PMP claim against official sources.

I found this PMP claim: [paste claim]. Help me verify it using official PMI and Pearson VUE sources only. Build a table with the claim, the official source to check, exact page or section if available, current wording summary, last checked date, and whether the claim is confirmed, contradicted, or still uncertain. If you cannot verify it from an official source, say so clearly.

What to paste

  • The claim you saw and where you saw it.
  • Whether it affects eligibility, scheduling, fees, exam format, or study content.
  • Any official links you already opened.

Expected output

  • A claim-by-claim verification table.
  • Official PMI or Pearson VUE source targets.
  • A clear "do not act yet" flag for unverified claims.

Next step

After policy facts are verified, return to the PMP Study Hub and update only the study actions affected by the verified fact.

Use case: Verify the current PMP exam version or outline.

I need to verify whether my PMP materials match the current PMP exam information. Create a research checklist for checking PMI's current PMP certification page, the active examination content outline, candidate resources, and any update notices. Compare my material dates and topic labels against official source wording. Do not decide from memory; mark every unsupported point as unverified.

What to paste

  • Book, course, or question bank publication dates.
  • Outline version or domain labels shown in your material.
  • Any official PMI page titles or links you found.

Expected output

  • An official-outline verification checklist.
  • Material dates and topic labels that need review.
  • A conservative decision on whether to keep, supplement, or replace material.

Next step

Use PMP 2026 exam version review as a study context, then confirm official details directly with PMI.

Use case: Detect outdated PMP study materials.

I am checking whether my PMP study materials may be outdated. Build a review table that compares publication date, exam outline references, agile and hybrid coverage, business environment coverage, terminology, and exam policy claims. Separate content-age risk from official-policy risk. Tell me which points require PMI confirmation before I rely on them.

What to paste

  • Material name, edition, publication date, and course date.
  • Topics that look missing or old.
  • Any claims about eligibility, fees, or exam logistics.

Expected output

  • A risk table for content, terminology, and policy claims.
  • A keep/supplement/replace recommendation with reasons.
  • Official checks for any rule-dependent statements.

Next step

Use PMP practice questions to test whether the material gap is hurting scenario judgment.

Use case: Compare PMP training providers.

I am comparing PMP training providers. Create a comparison matrix that separates eligibility documentation, current exam coverage, instructor or support model, practice question quality, update policy, refund or access terms, time commitment, and cost. Do not claim a provider is PMI-approved unless I provide official evidence. Mark claims that require checking with PMI or the provider's official page.

What to paste

  • Provider names and official course links.
  • Price, duration, access period, and certificate wording.
  • Your budget, study schedule, and support needs.

Expected output

  • A comparison matrix with evidence columns.
  • Questions to ask before paying.
  • A shortlist based on your constraints, not marketing claims.

Next step

After choosing materials, build a schedule with PMP study prompts.

Use case: Check PMI and Pearson VUE policy questions.

I have a PMP policy question about [paste topic: eligibility, scheduling, rescheduling, cancellation, ID, accommodations, online testing, test center, break rules, or retake]. Build a verification workflow that tells me which questions belong to PMI, which belong to Pearson VUE, what official pages to check, what exact wording to save, and what I should not do until the rule is confirmed.

What to paste

  • The policy question and why it matters.
  • Your appointment status, if relevant.
  • Any official-source text you already found.

Expected output

  • A PMI versus Pearson VUE responsibility split.
  • Exact evidence to capture before acting.
  • A conservative next action and escalation path.

Next step

Use PMP exam operations prompts to turn verified policy into an exam-day checklist.

Use case: Summarize official PMP source text.

I will paste text from an official PMI or Pearson VUE page. Summarize it for a PMP candidate without adding new facts. Extract the action items, deadlines or timing references, who controls the rule, what evidence I should save, and what remains unclear. If the pasted text does not answer the question, say that it does not answer the question.

What to paste

  • Official PMI or Pearson VUE text copied from the page.
  • Your specific question.
  • The page URL and date you checked it.

Expected output

  • A no-added-facts summary.
  • Candidate action items and open questions.
  • A source log you can keep with your exam notes.

Next step

Once the official source is understood, decide whether it changes your PMP mock exam timing or final review plan.

Example use

Example input and output summary.

Example input: "A forum says PMP online exam rules changed this year. I have an appointment next month." Example output should separate PMI certification policy from Pearson VUE delivery policy, list official pages to check, require current wording, and refuse to treat the forum post as final evidence.

Common mistakes

Do not ask an AI tool to "tell me the current PMP rule" without giving it official source text. Do not rely on cached summaries, old videos, or user comments. Do not treat provider marketing copy as the same thing as PMI or Pearson VUE policy.

Best next action

Build a source log before changing your exam plan. Record the official page, the exact wording, and the date checked. If the rule affects payment, scheduling, ID, accommodations, or eligibility, verify directly with the official provider.
