About LicenseAtlas PMP

Project management exam practice for CAPM and PMP learners.

LicenseAtlas PMP focuses on project management question banks, mock exam sessions, study articles, and local progress tracking for CAPM candidates building fundamentals and PMP candidates preparing for scenario-based leadership questions.

What We Provide

LicenseAtlas PMP is an independent learning tool for project management exam preparation. The current site is centered on PMP scenario practice, PMP study guides, and exam-focused review articles, with CAPM-oriented beginner content planned around core vocabulary, process logic, and project management fundamentals.

CAPM content is designed for project management newcomers, students, coordinators, and less experienced practitioners who need a structured starting point. PMP content is designed for people with project management experience who lead or manage projects and need to answer judgment-heavy questions across people, process, and business environment topics.

Important Disclaimer

LicenseAtlas PMP is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Project Management Institute (PMI). CAPM, PMP, PMI, PMBOK, and related names are used only to identify learning categories and exam topics.

The practice questions and articles on this site are independent study materials. They are not official PMI exam questions and should be used alongside the current PMI exam content outline and other official candidate resources.
