About Us

Free short-drill training for overseas credential exams.

LicenseAtlas helps learners prepare for overseas license and credential exams through focused question practice, chapter-based drills, and local progress tracking.

What We Provide

LicenseAtlas is an independent learning tool for short-term training. The site organizes practice content across medical and nursing, finance and accounting, engineering and IT, and law and public service programs.

Our goal is to make high-friction credential preparation easier to start: choose a program, answer practice questions, review explanations, and track local progress in your browser.
