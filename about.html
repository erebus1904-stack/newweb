About PassGrid

Project management exam practice for CAPM and PMP learners.

PassGrid focuses on project management question banks, knowledge-point practice, study articles, and local progress tracking for CAPM candidates building fundamentals and PMP candidates preparing for scenario-based leadership questions.

What We Provide

PassGrid is an independent learning tool for project management exam preparation. The current site is centered on CAPM and PMP practice, study guides, and exam-focused review articles across project management fundamentals, predictive work, agile, business analysis, and scenario judgment.

CAPM content is designed for project management newcomers, students, coordinators, and less experienced practitioners who need a structured starting point. PMP content is designed for people with project management experience who lead or manage projects and need to answer judgment-heavy questions across people, process, and business environment topics.

Our editorial standards, correction process, and source-use approach are described in the Editorial Policy.

How The Site Is Organized

The practice workspace is built around two tracks. CAPM practice emphasizes vocabulary, project roles, life cycles, predictive planning, agile basics, and business analysis. PMP practice emphasizes judgment in realistic situations, including stakeholder conflict, change control, risk response, communication, delivery approach, and business value.

Study guide pages are written to support the question bank rather than replace it. A learner can read a guide to understand a topic, answer a focused set of questions, then return to the same guide when repeated mistakes show a weak concept or decision rule.

Question And Article Maintenance

Questions are reviewed for domain coverage, answer structure, explanation quality, and duplicate wording. The site also keeps checks for advertising code, analytics code, sitemap entries, article length, local links, and retired pages from older site directions.

When exam outlines or candidate rules change, PassGrid pages are updated to point learners back to official PMI and Pearson VUE sources before they make scheduling or final study decisions.

Important Disclaimer

PassGrid is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Project Management Institute (PMI). CAPM, PMP, PMI, PMBOK, and related names are used only to identify learning categories and exam topics.

The practice questions and articles on this site are independent study materials. They are not official PMI exam questions and should be used alongside the current PMI exam content outline and other official candidate resources.
